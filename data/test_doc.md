## What is RAG

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is a technique that combines retrieval and generation.
It first retrieves relevant documents from a knowledge base, then uses an LLM to generate answers.

## Benefits

- Reduces hallucination by grounding answers in real documents
- Provides citation sources for verification
- Supports incremental knowledge updates
